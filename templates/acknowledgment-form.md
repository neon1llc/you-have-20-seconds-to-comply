---
title: "Training and policy acknowledgment"
description: "A one-page form for staff to confirm they completed training and read the policies that apply to them. Copy it into your own letterhead and fill in the blanks."
reviewed: 2026-11-12
---

Copy this form, replace the parts in brackets, and keep each signed copy with the training record. A signature from an e-signature tool or a checkbox in a form tool works if it records who signed and when.

---

**[Business name]**
**Training and policy acknowledgment**

Name: ______________________________

Role: ______________________________

Training completed: [Topic, for example "HIPAA privacy and security"]

Date of training: ____________   Format: [session / self-study / workshop]   Length: ________

Led by: ______________________________

**Policies covered**

| Policy | Version or date |
|---|---|
| [Privacy policy] | [2026-11-01] |
| [Information security policy] | [2026-11-01] |
| [Acceptable use policy] | [2026-11-01] |
| [Incident reporting procedure] | [2026-11-01] |

**I confirm that:**

1. I completed the training named above.
2. I have read the policies listed above, or been shown where to find them, and I understand what they ask of me.
3. I know who to contact with questions: [name, title, phone, email].
4. I know how to report a suspected incident or mistake: [how].
5. I understand that I should report mistakes promptly, and that not following these policies can lead to discipline under [business name]'s sanctions policy.

Signature: ______________________________   Date: ____________

---

**For the record keeper:** enter this acknowledgment in the [training log](training-log.csv) and keep the signed copy for at least as long as the strictest rule that applies to you (six years under HIPAA, for example).

## Sources

- HIPAA training documentation and six-year retention: [45 CFR § 164.530(b)(2)(ii) and (j)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.530).
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 requirement 12.6.3, annual acknowledgment of the security policy, from the PCI Security Standards Council [Document Library](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/).
- HIPAA sanctions policy: [45 CFR § 164.530(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.530).
