This domain is for drug, biologic, device, and supplement makers, contract manufacturers and labs, clinical research sites and CROs, and the IT providers and software vendors who support their regulated systems.

FDA inspects and enforces in the United States. The EMA and national authorities do in the EU.

These pages are training, not legal advice or a compliance determination.
