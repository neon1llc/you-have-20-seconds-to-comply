This domain is for public schools and districts, colleges, tutoring and after-school programs that work with schools, and the edtech, IT, and data companies that handle student records or serve children under 13.

The U.S. Department of Education enforces FERPA. The FTC and state attorneys general enforce COPPA. States, including New York, add their own student privacy laws.
