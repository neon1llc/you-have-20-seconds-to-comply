This domain is for small and midsize businesses building a security program, and anyone asked which framework they follow or whether they have a SOC 2 report.

Frameworks are voluntary, but laws such as the SHIELD Act and the FTC Act expect reasonable security, and customers and insurers ask for evidence of it.

These pages are training, not legal advice or a compliance determination.
