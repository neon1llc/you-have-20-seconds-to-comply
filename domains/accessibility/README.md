This domain is for businesses open to the public, health care providers, schools, state and local governments, and the agencies and developers who build their websites and apps.

The Department of Justice enforces the ADA, HHS enforces Section 504 for its funding recipients, and people with disabilities can sue under the ADA.

These pages are training, not legal advice or a compliance determination.
