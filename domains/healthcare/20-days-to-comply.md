Read [20 minutes to HIPAA](20-minutes-to-hipaa.md) first. Each day below is one to two hours of work for the person leading the effort. If a step is already done, check it and move on. The order matters more than the day numbers: inventory before risk, and risk before policy.

## The plan

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Name a privacy officer and a security officer. Write their names where staff can see them. |
| 2 | Decide whether you're a covered entity, a business associate, or both. Write down why. |
| 3 | List every system, device, and paper file that holds PHI. Include phones, email, backups, and the fax machine. |
| 4 | List every vendor that touches PHI. Mark which ones have a signed BAA. |
| 5 | Send BAAs to the vendors that lack one. Pause anything new with a vendor that won't sign. |
| 6 | Turn on full-disk encryption on every laptop and phone that touches PHI. |
| 7 | Turn on multi-factor authentication for email, the record system, and remote access. |
| 8 | End shared logins. Give each person their own account. |
| 9 | Start the risk analysis with the HHS SRA tool. Block two hours. |
| 10 | Finish the risk analysis. Pick the top five risks and assign an owner and a date to each. |
| 11 | Check that backups run, are encrypted, and can be restored. Restore one file to prove it. |
| 12 | Gather or write your core policies: privacy, security, breach response, sanctions, and records access. |
| 13 | Review your Notice of Privacy Practices. Post it in the office and on the website. |
| 14 | Write a one-page breach response plan: who to call, what to preserve, and the 60-day clock. |
| 15 | Schedule training for everyone. Use the [facilitator guide](../../training/facilitator-guide.md). |
| 16 | Run the training. Collect a signed [acknowledgment](../../templates/acknowledgment-form.md) from each person. |
| 17 | Record the training in the [training log](../../templates/training-log.csv). |
| 18 | Walk the office. Look for visible screens, open file rooms, and unlocked cabinets. Fix what you find. |
| 19 | Test how an access request flows from the front desk to completion. Time it. |
| 20 | Set the calendar: annual training, annual risk analysis review, quarterly access reviews, and BAA review. |

## After day 20

- **Every year:** workforce training and acknowledgments, a review of the risk analysis, and a review of policies and the Notice of Privacy Practices.
- **Every quarter:** a review of who has access to the record system, and a test restore from backup.
- **When anything changes:** update the risk analysis for new systems, offices, or vendors, sign a BAA before a new vendor touches PHI, and train affected staff on policy changes.
- **Keep for six years:** policies, risk analyses, training records, BAAs, and breach assessments.
