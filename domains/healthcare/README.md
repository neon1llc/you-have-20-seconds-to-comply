This domain is for medical, dental, and behavioral health practices, pharmacies, labs, health plans, and the IT, billing, and software vendors that handle their patient data.

The HHS Office for Civil Rights enforces HIPAA. State attorneys general can too.

These pages are training, not legal advice or a compliance determination.
