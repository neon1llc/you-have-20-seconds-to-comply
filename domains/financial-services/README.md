This domain is for tax preparers, accountants, mortgage brokers and lenders, auto dealers that finance or lease, check cashers, money transmitters, appraisers, settlement companies, debt collectors, colleges in federal student aid programs, and the vendors who serve them.

The Federal Trade Commission enforces the Safeguards Rule for non-bank financial institutions. Banks and credit unions answer to their own regulators.
