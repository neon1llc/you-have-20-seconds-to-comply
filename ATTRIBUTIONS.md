# Attributions

You Have 20 Seconds To Comply is written in its own words. Each page lists the sources it relies on in a Sources section. This page explains how outside material is used.

## Public domain, cited and summarized

U.S. federal statutes, regulations, Federal Register documents, and publications of federal agencies are public domain. The guides summarize them and occasionally quote short passages, and link to the official text, mostly on [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/), the [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/), and agency websites (HHS, FDA, FTC, FCC, DOJ, the Department of Education, NIST, and CISA).

State laws and regulations are cited from official state sites where available, such as the [New York State Senate](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation) for New York statutes.
